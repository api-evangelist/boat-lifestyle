Question: Is boAt only an audio brand? Answer : No, boAt is a multi-category Indian consumer electronics brand. While personal audio (TWS earbuds and headphones) remains a core segment, boAt’s catalogue spans smartwatches, portable Google TV projectors, Dolby soundbars, party speakers, personal care trimmers, dashcams, and fast-charging power solutions.
