India's leading audio and wearables brand, boAt, has introduced the Rockerz 378, a groundbreaking neckband featuring 3D Spatial Bionic Sound tuned by THX™. This flagship model boasts large dynamic drivers, a dedicated gaming mode for low-latency audio, and an impressive 25-hour battery life with boAt ASAP charge. Crafted by boAt Labs, the Rockerz 378 showcases exceptional build quality with a soft, skin-friendly silicone exterior over a lightweight frame for all-day comfort.
