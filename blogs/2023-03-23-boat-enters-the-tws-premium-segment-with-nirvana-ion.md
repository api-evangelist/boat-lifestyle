India’s #1 audio and wearables brand, boAt (Imagine Marketing), is delighted to launch its first premium product in the TWS segment — the one-of-a-kind ‘Nirvana Ion’ TWS Earbuds. Immersive sound with technologies filled to the brim, this new wireless earbud is all ready to send the competition back to its drawing board. Introducing the Nirvana Ion, the ‘Next Big Thing’ from boAt, is the first-ever TWS by the brand which has 24hrs of earbuds playback.
